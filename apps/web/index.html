Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You play in your browser for free, with nothing to download or install.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Phones play sideways with touch controls made for thumbs: two dials, a fire mode you choose, and buttons you can move and fade. Tablets and computers work too.

How do I play with my friends?

Choose Create room and send the link, or read out the six-letter code. Up to eight people play in a room, and bots fill any empty seats so two of you still get a real match.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a name and play. Your soldier and settings are kept on your device.

Will it run on an older computer or phone?

It picks graphics to suit your device and keeps adjusting them while you play. Low graphics, Reduce motion and Reduce transparency in Settings make it lighter still.

Can I use a controller?

Yes: PlayStation and Xbox controllers work on computers and Android phones, with the buttons shown on screen.
