Privacy

Updated 28 October 2026 · plain words first, details below

In short. We keep what the game needs to run matches. Only if you say yes, we also use your play data (how you play) to improve the game and to pick the ads you see on the billboards in the maps. Today those ads are all our own: Deet Studios, the studio that makes %GAME_NAME%, and WorldStreet, another of our products. We never keep your IP address, never use third-party trackers, never fingerprint your device and never sell or share your data. You can say no, change your mind, or delete your data in Settings → Privacy.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhyWhen
Your player: the name you pick, a random player id, your level and XP, your saved look and guns, your friends To let you play, keep your progress and find friends Always (you can delete your player)
Match stats: in online matches, your kills, deaths, damage, the guns you used, how long you lived and played, when you left, your ping band and reconnects, with your player id To run matches, keep your level and the leaderboards, and balance the weapons and maps Always: the game needs them
Play data: when you play and for how long, the screens you reach, the features you use, your device type (phone, tablet or computer; touch, keys or a controller; the graphics level) and your time zone's offset, tied to a random id that lasts one visit and to your player id To see where the game confuses or loses people and fix it, and to pick which of our billboard ads you see more often (for example ads made for touch players, or for evening players) Only if you say yes. We ask everyone, and you can turn it off any time
Counts with no id at all: errors on your device (a screen that didn't load) and which billboard ads were on screen, saved and visited To fix bugs and report to advertisers how often their ads were seen, never who saw them Unless your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track (and in Europe, ad counts only with your yes)
Your age answer: that you said you are 18 or older, the year you were born and when you said it (never your birthday) %GAME_NAME% is for adults (Terms §1): online play and saved accounts need it On your device before your first online match; with your account when you save one
Your country, worked out from the connection or your time zone To follow each country's rules and see where players come from With the match stats and play data above. The address itself is looked up and dropped, never stored

We don't collect your real name, email (unless you add one to your account), messages, contacts, location beyond your country, or anything about you from other sites. Billboard ads in the game never open anything during a match, and the ads we show are never chosen from what other companies know about you. If we ever show paid ads from other companies, your play data will never be used to aim them at you (Terms §7).

How long we keep it

  • Single events (one match, one visit): 90 days, then deleted.
  • Your daily summary (days you played, matches, the device you used most): 13 months, then deleted.
  • Totals with no player in them (for example "1,240 matches on Fog Stair this week"): kept to see trends.
  • Your player: until you delete it. A guest player nobody has used for a long time is removed.
  • Problem reports you send: 7 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Say no or yes to sharing your play data: the card on Home the first time, then Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete my data (Settings → Privacy): deletes your play data from our servers (single events within minutes) and this device's event log. Tick "Also delete my player" to delete your name, level, friends and match history too.
  • You can also ask to see, correct or move your data, or object to how we use it. Contact: %PRIVACY_CONTACT%.
  • If you think we got something wrong you can complain to your data protection authority: the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, South Africa's Information Regulator, or the authority where you live in the EU or UK.

Under 18?

%GAME_NAME% is for adults, 18 and over (Terms §1). If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data; a parent or guardian can tell us through the contact above. We never use play data to aim paid ads at anyone, and ads in the game are never about gambling, alcohol, tobacco or other adult products, except where marked 18+ and switched off in places that require it.

Who sees it, and where it is

Only the people who make %GAME_NAME% can see play data and match stats, as totals on a private page. They are stored on our own server (Microsoft Azure, South Africa North, in Johannesburg). No analytics company, ad network or data broker receives them. We protect it with encrypted connections and keep access to our team.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and ask again where your yes is needed.
