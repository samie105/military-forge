Privacy

Updated 29 October 2026 · plain words first, details below

In short. We keep what the game needs to run: your player, your matches, your account if you save one, and your purchases if you make any. Only if you say yes, we also use your play data (how you play) to improve the game and to pick the ads you see on the billboards in the maps. Today those ads are all our own: Deet Studios, the studio that makes %GAME_NAME%, and WorldStreet, another of our products. With your player we keep only the first part of your network address, never the whole of it. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell your data. You can say no, change your mind, or delete your data in Settings → Privacy.

Why we're allowed to use it

  • To give you the game you asked for: your player, account, match stats, purchases and your age answer.
  • Because you said yes: play data, and choosing our ads from it. You can take your yes back any time; that doesn't undo what was done before.
  • To keep the game safe and working: problem reports, counts with no id, the network-address counters, and server logs.
  • To keep the records a business must keep: payments and refunds.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your name, level and look in menus and matches, hear you on voice chat, and can open your profile: your level, lifetime stats, kills by gun and recent matches. Leaderboards show your name and scores. Only your friends see whether you're online and which room you're in.
  • The people who make %GAME_NAME% see play data and match stats as totals on a private page, and read problem reports.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts our server, in South Africa North (Johannesburg). Everything above is stored there.
  • Flutterwave takes payments. When you buy Brass, we send it your account email, your in-game name and the amount. You pay on Flutterwave's own page, and it handles your card or bank details under its own privacy policy. We never see them.
  • If we email you about your account (confirming your email, or a password reset), the email goes out through Resend, which gets your email address.
  • A bot check can appear when you save an account, use Forgot password, sign in after several wrong passwords, or send a problem report. It is Cloudflare Turnstile: Cloudflare sees your network address and details of your browser. When a room connection fails and the game sends its own problem report, it runs the same check in the background first, and sends nothing if the check doesn't pass.
  • In a local room (players on the same network), your device asks a Google server for your network's public address, so the devices can find each other.
  • No analytics company, ad network or data broker receives your data, and we never sell it.

Some of these companies work outside your country and outside South Africa, including in the United States, so your data can be handled there. We use each one only for the job above.

On your device

The game keeps these in your browser's storage: your settings and controls, your sign-in, your age answer, your play-data answer, your progress through the tour and tips with a short log of them (it never leaves your device), voice settings, ads you saved (for a day), how often the "install the game" offer has shown, and copies of the game's files so it loads fast. We set one cookie, to keep you signed in, and no advertising or tracking cookies. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it from your device.

How long we keep it

  • Single events (one match, one visit): 90 days, then deleted.
  • Your daily summary (days you played, matches, the device you used most): 13 months, then deleted.
  • Totals with no player in them (for example "1,240 matches on Fog Stair this week"): kept to see trends. Hourly totals: 5 weeks.
  • Your player and account, with your match history, Brass and items: until you delete your player.
  • A finished match's record keeps the name and stats of everyone in it, so the name you played under stays in that record after you delete your player.
  • Payment records: kept after you delete your player, without your player attached, as our record of the money. So is any change we make to a balance by hand, with its reason.
  • Signed-in devices: a device's sign-in is deleted 7 days after it ends (you sign out, it runs out, or you use Sign out everywhere).
  • Links we email you (confirming your email, resetting your password): deleted 7 days after they are used or run out.
  • Problem reports: 7 days.
  • Network-address counters: an hour at most.
  • Server logs can hold the first part of your network address next to account events (signing in, saving an account, changing a password), and the full address when a fast connection fails to start. They have no fixed end date yet.
  • Backups: a copy of the database is made every night and the last 7 are kept, so data you delete can stay in a backup for up to 7 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Say no or yes to sharing your play data: the card on Home the first time, then Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete my data (Settings → Privacy): deletes your play data from our servers (single events within minutes) and this device's event log, and turns sharing off. Your player and match stats stay.
  • Tick Also delete my player to delete your name, level, friends, match history, account, Brass and items too. Brass and items deleted this way can't be refunded or brought back, so refund unspent Brass first if you want the money back.
  • Change your name in the game, and end your sessions with Sign out everywhere (Settings → Account).
  • You can also ask to see your data or get a copy of it, correct it, or object to how we use it. Contact: %PRIVACY_CONTACT%. Tell us your in-game name so we can find your player.
  • If you think we got something wrong you can complain to your data protection authority: the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, South Africa's Information Regulator, or the authority where you live in the EU or UK.

Under 18?

%GAME_NAME% is for adults, 18 and over (Terms §1). If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data; a parent or guardian can tell us through the contact above. We never use play data to aim paid ads at anyone. Our own WorldStreet ads are about trading: they carry "Trading involves risk. 18+." and can be switched off in places that require it.

Keeping it safe

Every connection to the game is encrypted, passwords are kept only as one-way hashes, and access to the server is kept to our team. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you, and the authorities the law requires us to tell.
