Military Forge: motion data credits

Some of the soldiers' animations are made from motion capture, retargeted and restyled for the game's characters.

100STYLE
  100STYLE dataset by Ian Mason, Sebastian Starke and Taku Komura, licensed under CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/
  Used for: the walk (changed: retargeted, restyled, re-timed, feet re-planted).

CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
  The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
  The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
  Used for: the idles (changed: retargeted, restyled, looped, feet re-planted).
