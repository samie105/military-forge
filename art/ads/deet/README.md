# DEET billboard masters

Masters for DEET's in-world billboards (Street Market), at docs/14 §3.1's upload sizes (Screens
2048×1536, Hoardings 2304×384). `apps/web/scripts/deet-ads-masters.mjs` draws them, and
`apps/web/scripts/house-ads.py --sponsored-only` grades them into the runtime layers.

## Who DEET is (what the copy stands on)

DEET is a Lagos studio (DEET LTD) that works like a creative agency and builds like an engineering
team. It has three crafts under one roof: brand and identity, software (websites, web and mobile
apps, backends), and AI and automation (assistants, agents and workflow automation). It runs on its
own AI: Alma takes every new brief before a person picks it up. The way it works is the pitch:
something real to click by the end of week one, a new version every week, dates set before the
work starts, and a team that stays after launch ("the E in DEET": Developing & Evolving Everything
Tech). It also builds worlds: DEET makes this game. Source: the deet.studio site code and its brand
book. No clients, numbers or awards are claimed, because the site doesn't show any yet.

## Brand rules used here (DEET's brand book)

- Ink #0B0B0E, bone #F3F1EA, ash #8D8C93. Bone-on-ink is the main colourway. Ash with ink type is
  the second colourway, because a bone board would outshine the street (docs/14 §3.4, ≤ 0.48).
- The DEET. mark (`brand/deet-mark.svg`, the site's own file). The colour ramp appears only in
  its dot, never as a background or type. That also keeps the boards clear of the game's
  signal hues.
- Bricolage Grotesque bold, set tight, for headlines (`brand/fonts`, OFL; the site's own Latin
  subset). One idea per board. The answer line is ash, after the site's lit/muted headlines.
- No URL, QR code or call to action on the art (docs/14 §1.3 rule 8, §3.2). deet.studio travels
  with tap-to-save.

## The copy (26 Oct 2026)

★ marks the lines on the boards. Each headline has at most 6 words and one optional answer line.

**The studio: who DEET is**
- ★ *The brand, the app, the AI.* / One studio makes all three. The whole offer in one
  sentence. Concrete nouns, no adjectives.
- ★ *AI with taste.* / A design studio that builds AI. The AI-agency position, with the craft
  that makes it different. Three words read in a glance.
- ★ *Every project starts with Alma.* / Our AI listens. Our team builds. It shows the AI work
  instead of claiming it, and it is true on every project.
- *Intelligence, designed.* / AI products and agents, by DEET. Same idea as "AI with taste" but
  colder, so it wasn't picked.

**The proof: how DEET works**
- ★ *Clickable by Friday.* / Not a deck. Not a document. Week one, said in the client's terms.
- ★ *Shipped Friday. Sharper by Monday.* / We stay on after launch. The weekly releases and
  the after-launch care.
- *Our dates are promises.* / Set before the work starts. True, but reads as a defence.
- *Less deck. More demo.* / Something real in week one. Overlaps "Clickable by Friday".

**In the game: a wink to where it's seen**
- ★ *We build worlds too.* / You're standing in one. The player is inside the proof.
- ★ *Let agents do the grind.* / AI that handles the busywork. Gamer slang for repetitive work,
  turned into the AI pitch.
- ★ *Built for the rush.* / Quick on any phone, any network. The site's promise, read twice in a
  match.
- *Low ping. High standards.* / Made by the studio behind this game. Latency depends on the
  player's network, so it's a claim we can't always back.

No line talks about fighting, weapons or winning by force.
